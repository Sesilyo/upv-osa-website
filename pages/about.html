What is OSA?

In support of the academic programs of the University, the Office of Student Affairs or OSA, headed by the Director, is committed to the holistic development of students. It has main office located at the College Union Building (CUB) in the Miag-ao, and an extension office in the Iloilo City campus. It delivers an integrated program of services to meet the students' multifarious needs throughout the two campuses (Miag-ao and Iloilo City).

Major Services Offered:

  • Guidance & Counseling
  • Student Organizations & Counseling
  • Scholarships & Financial Assistance
  • Residential Services
